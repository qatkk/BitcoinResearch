### Notes While Reading the Paper

- The signature scheme is a weak partially-blind signing. Meaning that the message is hidden from the signer (and not the signature) and that some properties on the message being signed are proven to the signer (so some information is given away from the message).
**This might affect the linkability of the signatures and anonomity of the users**
